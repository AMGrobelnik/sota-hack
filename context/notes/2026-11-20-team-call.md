TITLE: ['Dialysis graft simulation ideas']

Hackathon Idea: Dialysis Graft Geometry Simulation
Core concept: simulate blood flow through arteriovenous graft geometries to find optimal shapes
Dialysis patients need artery-vein connections 3x/week; synthetic grafts fail ~50% within a year
Failure point is the venous anastomosis, where geometry drives turbulent flow and intimal hyperplasia
Medicare spent $48.9B on dialysis in 2022; access failures are a major cost driver
Approach: GPU-parallel search across 10,000 candidate geometries, ranked by flow stability
Reynolds number at dialysis flow rates (~1,000+) means flow becomes unstable, needs careful modeling
Agents iterate on graft shapes, optimizing for smooth laminar flow at the join
Vascular Model Repository (open-source) has cardiovascular anatomy models; published brachial artery waveforms in AVF patients available for boundary conditions
Concerns raised by Claude:
900 GPU hours needed (manageable with sponsor credits + ~130GB VRAM available)
Flow instability at dialysis rates is a real modeling challenge
No AI-for-science loop yet (needs iterative improvement mechanism)

Staying focused on dialysis AVF grafts is cleaner
