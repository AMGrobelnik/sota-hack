# Past SoTA hackathons and ARIA summits

What is known about earlier SoTA winners and ARIA's summits. Only
items from an opened page count as confirmed; first-pass claims that
did not survive a second check are listed as dropped. Raw findings:
[data/past-events.json](data/past-events.json).

## Past SoTA hackathon winners

- **Rebooting State Capacity** (London, 26-27 Apr 2025). First
  Treasury Busters (£1,000): one live source of truth for building
  and approving business cases for major spending. Second DVSA
  Reimagined (£500): a simpler first government contact for young
  people getting a driving licence. Third A1 Voice (£250, also the
  £1,000 Basis Prize): smart voicemail for NHS hospitals handling over
  10,000 daily calls, engaged with 17 departments across 6 trusts.
  Honourable mentions: SCYLLA (seabed sensing), Hacknee (prediction
  markets for council decisions), Fighting Crime One Query at a Time,
  Pranav (visual mapping without GPS), Seaguard (small-boat
  detection). Source:
  <https://sotaletters.substack.com/p/some-updates-from-sota>
- **Human Augmentation** (London, July 2025). Prizes £1,000 plus a
  week of office space, £500, £250. Judging asked: is it better than
  other methods, would it be worse without the AI, would it be worse
  without a human. Winners not confirmed. Source:
  <https://luma.com/1au1lxbt>
- **Defence tech hack** (London, 2025). Winners Pia Park and Enrico
  Bottazzi ("Friend or Foe: from theory to battlefield"). A search
  snippet, not confirmed, says the project was Kolu, a drone
  friend-or-foe identifier for Ukrainian forces. Source:
  <https://sotaletters.substack.com/p/letters-vi>
- **Robotics hack** (2025). Winners James Baghurst and Calum Drysdale
  ("Becoming Hardware Scientists"). Project not described. Same
  source.
- **Mechinterp hack** (2025). Winners Nitish Mital and Simon Malzard
  ("Explainability-guided synthetic data"). Project not described.
  Same source.
- **Weather Control and Geoengineering** (London, 31 May-1 Jun 2025).
  A 12-year-old won the Novelty Prize; overall winners not found.
  Same source.
- **Trust Everything, Everywhere** (SoTA with ARIA, London, date not
  stated). Prize categories Grand, Technical Excellence, Novelty; ARIA
  programme director Alex Obadia spoke. Winners not published.
  Source: <https://luma.com/eron45y6>

Dropped as unconfirmed: Human Augmentation winners Thomas.Orb,
EchoSight, everything2prompt; Mine Trust as Trust Everything,
Everywhere grand prize.

Not found: judges for any event, winners' code or write-ups. The
evidence is too thin to find a pattern in what wins.

## ARIA summits

- **AI for Science Summit 2026**, Manchester, 26 Nov 2026,
  12:00-18:00, with Conception X and Turing Innovation Catalyst
  Manchester. Second edition. Agenda, speakers and demo format not
  yet published. This is where the first prize's demo slot is.
  Source: <https://allai.events/event/ai-for-science-summit-2026>
- **AI for Science: Scientists to Builders**, London, probably 10 Nov
  2025 (likely the first edition; that link is inference). Sixteen
  sessions, investor fireside chats, lightning pitches; no demos
  published. Sources: <https://luma.com/edg3o5u2>,
  <https://ariaresearch.substack.com/p/exploring-ai-scientists-the-potential>
- **ARIA Summit 2025** (community summit, recap 2 Jun 2025). Talks and
  panels only, no demos. Source:
  <https://aria.org.uk/insights/inside-the-aria-summit>
