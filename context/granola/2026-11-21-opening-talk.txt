Sponsor Presentations
Fractal (frontier AI chip company)
Core thesis: speed is the primary dividend in AI; memory bandwidth is the bottleneck
Flops have scaled ~1,000,000x over 40 years; memory bandwidth only ~40x
Approach: stack DRAM on top of logic to maximize memory-to-compute bandwidth
Contrasts with SRAM-based competitors (Groq, Cerebras): fast but expensive and poor at long contexts
Goal: combine capacity and bandwidth to rewrite economics of inference at long context lengths
Full-stack team: silicon architecture, verification, ML research, process engineering, test and bring-up
Hardware targeting large-scale volume by end of next year
2 Fractal team members present; Clara available on the day to help participants
Inherent (AI for science, human-machine collaboration)
Thesis: AI should augment human scientists, not replace them; focus on optimal human-model teaming
Current text-based AI interfaces don’t reflect how science is actually done
Building “living collaborator” models: proactive, curious, self-modifying interaction surfaces
Organizational infrastructure focus: model has same permissions as human researchers, decides when to interrupt vs. act autonomously
Training own models rather than bolting tools onto frontier models
Key result: 27B model outperformed frontier models on replicating ML papers (both general ML and AI-for-science benchmarks)
Example win: model correctly sampled from latent space for molecule generation; frontier models just sampled from training data
Handles model staleness by training a small “tool-use and planning” model that delegates coding tasks to the best available external coding agents
Hiring across post-training, infrastructure, and scientific domains
