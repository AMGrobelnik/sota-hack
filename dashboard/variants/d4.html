What a run does

  1. Spec. Reads your brief and fixes the clinical line and gate budget.
  2. Reference. Trains a large float model: the accuracy ceiling.
  3. Propose. The Architect writes a batch of about 3,000 chip designs.
  4. Train. The whole batch trains at once as one GPU job.
  5. Compile. The best 16 become gate circuits with weights wired in.
  6. Verify. Circuit against model, bit for bit, on unseen patients.
  7. Report. A datasheet, the netlist and the check log.

Propose to Verify repeats in rounds. You can pause and redirect at any point.
