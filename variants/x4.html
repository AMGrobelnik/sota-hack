Inside the chip.

Sensor signals come in on one side. A small fine-tuned AI model sits in the middle. One prediction goes out the other side.

Smaller, then smaller.

AI scientists fine-tune thousands of candidate models at once, then redraw the chip around the smallest one that is good enough.

The model, as gates.

The kept model is compiled into logic gates with its weights wired in, then checked against the model.

Why smaller matters.

Same job, a smaller chip. The package is redrawn around the smaller circuit.

  • Uses less power
  • Lasts longer on a battery
  • Costs less to make
  • Fits inside the device

One chip per device.

Every run returns a different chip, sized and packaged for its own device. Pick one to read what it was asked to detect.
