One loop, run until the chip is small enough.

  1. Describe the device and what it must detect.
  2. Propose chip designs.
  3. Fine-tune thousands at once.
  4. Compile weights wired into gates.
  5. Check circuit against model; round again while a smaller design might pass.
  6. Return the smallest chip that does the job.

Every candidate model in a round is fine-tuned together, in one GPU batch, not one after another.
