What the AI scientists do

  • Propose chip designs for your device.
  • Fine-tune thousands of small candidate models at once.
  • Compile the best into logic gates, weights wired in.
  • Check each circuit against its model.

The loop, in short.

One search, run by AI scientists, from a plain description to a finished chip.

Where the speed comes from: every candidate model in a round is fine-tuned together, in one GPU batch, not one after another.
