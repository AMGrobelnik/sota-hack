How many logic gates does a diagnosis need?

A medical sensing device is sensors, a housing and a core chip. The chip takes the sensor signal and does one job. We look for the smallest chip that does it.

  • SmallOnly the gates the job needs, and nothing else on the chip.
  • Low powerA fixed circuit with no processor running a program.
  • Works offlineIt gives its answer on the device, with no connection.

It outputs only the answer. There is no software that an update could change to send raw data out, so the patient's signal never leaves the device.

An environment, not a chip company.

You write a prompt naming the device and its job. AI agents take it from there, and you can watch every stage, pause and redirect.

  1. SpecYour prompt: the device and the one job its chip does.
  2. ReferenceA reference model that the chip has to match.
  3. ProposeAI agents propose chip designs.
  4. Train (batch)Thousands of candidates train at once, as one GPU batch.
  5. CompileThe best become a circuit of logic gates with the weights wired in.
  6. VerifyThe circuit is checked bit for bit against the model.
  7. ReportYou get back the smallest chip that does the job.
