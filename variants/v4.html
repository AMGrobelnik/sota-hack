How many logic gates does a diagnosis need?

That is the question behind the project. The finished device holds no AI model: AI is used to find the circuit, then it is gone.

  • Small and low power

    A wired circuit of logic gates needs little room and little energy.

  • Works offline

    It does its job on the device, with no network and no phone.

  • Only the answer comes out

    There is no software that an update could change to send raw data out.

The patient's signal never leaves the device. The chip reads it, gives one answer, and keeps nothing else to send.

Sensors, a housing and one small chip.

A medical sensing device has three parts. The chip at its core takes the sensor signal and does one job.

  • HousingHolds the parts together.
  • Core chipTakes the sensor signal and gives one answer. This is the part we design.
  • SensorsPick up the signal, such as an ECG or an EEG.

An environment, not a chip company. You describe the device. AI agents find the smallest chip that does its job.
