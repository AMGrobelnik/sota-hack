    A medical sensing device

    Three parts: sensors, a housing, and a chip at its core. The chip takes the sensor signal and does one job.

    The sensors

    They pick up a signal from the body, such as the electrical rhythm of the heart, and pass it straight to the chip.

    The housing

    It holds the parts together against the skin. The patient's signal never leaves it.

    The core chip

    A wired chip: small, low power, and it works offline. It outputs only the answer. There is no software on it that an update could change to send raw data out.

    Logic gates, weights wired in

    How many logic gates does a diagnosis need? AI agents search for the smallest circuit that does the job, then check it bit for bit against the model.

    No AI model inside

    AI is used to find the circuit, then it is gone. The finished device holds only the gates.

Three steps from a sentence to a circuit you can check.
