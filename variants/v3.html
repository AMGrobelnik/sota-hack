How many logic gates does a diagnosis need?

We ask it for each device. The answer is the smallest chip that does the job.

  • Small

    Only the gates the job needs.

  • Low power

    No processor and no software to run.

  • Works offline

    It needs no network to give its answer.

The signal stays put.The chip outputs only the answer. There is no software an update could change to send raw data out.

No AI model on the device.AI is used to find the circuit. Then it is gone, and only the gates remain.

From a sentence to a circuit.

An environment, not a chip company. You write a prompt naming the device and its job. AI agents take it through seven stages, and you can watch every one, pause and redirect.

  1. Spec

    You name the device and its job.

  2. Reference

    A model of the job to check against.

  3. Propose

    AI agents propose chip designs.

  4. Train

    Thousands of candidates train at once, as one GPU batch.

  5. Compile

    The best becomes logic gates, weights wired in.

  6. Verify

    The circuit is checked bit for bit against the model.

  7. Report

    You get the smallest chip that does the job.

An early feasibility test.

On a laptop, Saturday 21 November 2026

One small heart-rhythm detector became 2,330 logic gates. The circuit matched its model on all 3,880 test windows. 256 candidate designs trained in one batch, at 1,626 per minute.

Accuracy is not yet at clinical level. This is an early feasibility test, not a clinical result.
