# Event facts

Scientific Parallelism hackathon. The central question: which
scientific questions could we investigate better if computation were
faster or ran at greater scale?

- **Organiser**: Society for Technological Advancement (SoTA).
- **Supporters**: Callosum, Inherent Labs, ARIA (the UK's Advanced
  Research and Invention Agency) and Fractile.
- **Page**: <https://luma.com/scientific-parallelism>
- **When**: Saturday 21 Nov 2026, 11:00, to Sunday 22 Nov, 18:00.
- **Where**: Halkin, 1-2 Paris Garden, London SE1 8ND.

## Sunday timetable

- 13:00 to 14:45: final hacking and demo prep.
- 14:45 to 15:00: judges' briefing.
- 15:00 to 16:30: demos and judging.
- 16:30 to 16:50: deliberation.
- 16:50 to 17:00: winners.

## Rules

- At most 4 people per team is recommended.
- Pre-written code is fine if the team says clearly what was done on
  the day.
- Own hardware is allowed.

## Compute

GPU environments and AI credits. Credits come through a link from the
organisers. The event page names no GPU model.

## Judging criteria

The organisers' slide, "What judges are looking for":

1. Correctness & assumptions: preserve intended behaviour; explain
   and justify simplifying assumptions.
2. Fundamental reshaping: rethink the problem to expose
   opportunities for parallelism.
3. Meaningful speedup: compare performance with a credible, relevant
   baseline.
4. Scientific value: show useful results, sound methods and
   convincing technical evidence.

Footer: informative negative results count too; a well-explained
failure to achieve a speedup can still win a prize.
